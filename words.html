1 Solar disk: The term solar disk refers to the visible, circular surface of the Sun when observed from a vantage point like Earth. It is essentially the bright, round shape of the Sun that we see in the sky during the daytime.

2 Umbra: The term umbra is commonly used in astronomy to describe the central and darkest part of a shadow, particularly in the context of eclipses. It is the region within a shadow where the source of light is completely blocked or obscured by an opaque object.

3. Penumbra: The penumbra is the outer, lighter part of the shadow where only a portion of the light source is obscured. It surrounds the umbra and represents a partial blocking of the light. In the context of a solar eclipse, the penumbra is the region on Earth from which a partial solar eclipse is visible, as the Moon only partially covers the Sun.
